## Verdict: FIX FIRST
Scores (1–10): Design 7 · Usability 5 · Creativity 8 · Content 4

## Blockers (must fix)

1. **[responsive / usability] On a real phone viewport, most cards don't fit their window.** At 375×667 (about iPhone Safari's `svh`), 15 of 16 stops overflow the window by 42–321 px ("Our life together" is 682 px tall in a 385 px window). The overflow goes into a nested scroller with no cue that it scrolls: text is cut mid-line (`…-375x667-transfer.png`). At 390×844, "The connection" still overflows by 19 px. The cause is fixed chrome totalling 274 px: the car card (67), the sign (~142) and the site bar (65).
   → While the stage is pinned on phones: hide `.bd-bar` (StoryRide.tsx:137-141 measures it; drop it instead), make the sign one row (ride.css:741-754: name plus two 48 px "Back"/"Next"), add `@media (height <= 720px)` rules beyond ride.css:655-663 (21:9 photos, or none on signs), and show a bottom fade with "More ↓" when `.bd-ride__platform` overflows.

2. **[a11y · WCAG 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (AA)] Map links take focus while off-screen.** `--map-shift` follows `--p` only (StoryRide.tsx:157-170 → ride.css:472 / :797) inside `overflow: hidden` (ride.css:217).
   - At 390, Shift+Tab back from station 13 focuses 9 of 16 map links at x = −58 … −506 px (`…-390x844-kbd-shifttab-offscreen.png`). At 1440, 2 links sit above the viewport.
   - Tabbing forward, the browser scrolls the hidden-overflow viewport itself (scrollLeft 237→518; scrollTop 154 at 1440) and never resets it, which desyncs the train marker.
   → Use `overflow: clip` on `.bd-ride-map__viewport`. On `focusin`, set `--map-shift` to centre the focused link; on `focusout`, hand control back to `--p`.

3. **[brief §4 guardrail / keyboard] Arrow keys don't move between stations.** The brief promises they do ("non-negotiable"). ArrowRight/ArrowDown on a map link scrolls the page instead (StoryRide.tsx:240-269 has no key handler). Tabbing through all 16 links is required to reach the card.
   → Roving tabindex: the current station gets `tabIndex=0`, the others `-1`. `onKeyDown` handles Arrow keys plus Home/End to move focus and call `go(i)`. This cuts 16 tab stops to 1.

4. **[detector] 15 findings, 0 waivers.** Home scans clean, so this change introduced all of them.
   - `side-tab` ×9: ride.css:79 (`.bd-stopcard` `border-top: 6px`) and ride.css:681 (`.bd-ride__sign`). → Give station cards the same full-width line plate the transfers already use (bullet plus "Brown Line" on the line colour, which is real CTA sign language) and drop the stripe. For the sign, drop the stripe; the car card already carries the line colour.
   - `content-hidden-at-rest` (error): 42% of the page's text sits at opacity 0 (ride.css:629, plus `inert` at StoryRide.tsx:290). → Waive in `.impeccable/config.json` with a written rationale, but only after should-fix 5 (a list view) exists, so the rationale is true.
   - `clipped-overflow-container` ×2 (stage, map viewport), `cramped-padding` (stage top), `repeating-stripes-gradient` (sleepers, ride.css:585): intentional. → Waive each with a one-line rationale in config. Fix blocker 2 first.
   - `tight-leading` 1.30: the licence credit wraps at `label-caps`. → Resolved by blocker 5.

5. **[17 px floor, PX-22] Text guests read or operate is at 13.8 px.** It gets through via the `bd-eyebrow` exemption, which is meant for ornamental labels above a title. The licence **link** (timeline-media.tsx:65, `target="_blank"` with no warning) and "Stand-in photo" (timeline-media.tsx:62) are a link and a fact the guest is owed, not ornament.
   → Set both in `body-sm` (17 px). Mark the new tab (`aria-label="… (opens in a new tab)"`) or drop `target`.

## Should fix (ranked: structural → motion → polish)

1. **[motion / usability] Nothing settles the ride on a station.** A fling or trackpad throw stops mid-travel about half the time, which leaves the guest on a ghosted frame. `DWELL = 0.5` (StoryRide.tsx:45) is equal to travel, not the "longer stretch" the brief asks for.
   → Put invisible snap sentinels in `.bd-ride__rail` at each plateau with `scroll-snap-align: start`, plus `html:has(.bd-ride[data-mode=ride]) { scroll-snap-type: y proximity }`. That is native, not jacking. Set DWELL to 0.6.
2. **[motion] Departing cards double-expose over arriving ones.** The passed card fades at `past × 3.2` while moving *toward* the camera (ride.css:624-629). For about 0.3 of a stop, an enlarged card at 40–60% opacity sits over the incoming card and the rails, text over text (`…-810x1080-07b-…`, `…-1440x900-07b-…`). This is the one janky frame.
   → Keep it opaque and move it out laterally with ease-in, no +Z. Or fade it by `past ≈ 0.12`. Never cross-fade text over text.
3. **[motion] Transfers are hidden behind the docked card.** With `--side: 0`, the next sign sits exactly behind the current card, so nothing ahead is visible when you dock (`…-1440x900-04-station-docked.png`). "The next memory grows out of the haze" only happens for stations.
   → Hang transfers above the track while ahead (negative Y × `--ahead`), as the comment at ride.css:602 already says.
4. **[performance] Long frames are ride-specific.** Evidence:
   - Unthrottled at 390 (dev build): 57/216 frames over 33 ms (p90 50 ms).
   - 4× CPU: p99 567 ms.
   - Home and the band below the ride at 4×: 1–2 long frames each.

   Headless software GL and dev-mode React inflate these numbers, so confirm on a production build on a mid-range Android. The causes are in the code:
   - (a) `frameFn` writes `--p`, then reads `offsetTop` / `clientHeight` / `scrollHeight`, forcing layout every frame (StoryRide.tsx:149-169). Cache the geometry on resize.
   - (b) `--p` is set on the section root, so everything inside restyles every frame. Set it only on the track, floor and map.
   - (c) `background-position` animates on the 220%×170% rotated floor and the skyline (ride.css:567, :588), a repaint every frame. Translate a tiled child instead.
   - (d) `will-change` is on all 16 stops (ride.css:631). Apply it only where |d| < 3.
   - (e) `el.dataset.line` is written every frame (StoryRide.tsx:155). Write it only on change.
5. **[usability / vestibular] No opt-out except an OS setting.** Most grandparents don't know `prefers-reduced-motion` exists. → Add a "Read it as a list" toggle in the intro and on the sign that switches to flat mode and persists. It is also the rationale that makes the content-hidden waiver honest.
6. **[usability, phone] The phone car card is 16 unlabelled dots.** "Tap a station on the map" means guessing. → Show the current and next names under the strip, or add an "All stations" button that opens the list.
7. **[consistency] The lede promises a map that flat mode hides.** Flat, reduced-motion and no-JS modes set the map to `display: none` (ride.css:360-362), but the lede still offers it (story.tsx:202). → In flat mode, render the car card as a static list of links, sticky at ≥1100 px. That also delivers the brief's "vertical strip map" with jump links.
8. **[motion] Back/Next reads as a cut, not a ride.** Visible travel lasts about 150 ms (p 8.06 → 8.70 → 9.00 within 100 ms), because the browser's smooth scroll spends half its distance inside the plateau. A 7-stop map jump strobes 7 cards in 750 ms. → Drive the scroll with rAF so p animates over ~800 ms on the train ease (invert `trainPosition`). For jumps of more than 2 stops, use a short fade-through.
9. **[screen reader] The arrival announcement fires while the guest is still in the hero.** "This is the Red Line: How we met." plays 600 ms after hydration, because `at` starts as `{index: 0, docked: true}` (StoryRide.tsx:93, :210-216). → Announce only once the stage is pinned.
10. **[find-in-page] Ctrl-F can't find 15 of 16 stops while riding.** `inert` removes their text from find-in-page (StoryRide.tsx:290), which contradicts the brief's "find-in-page keeps working". → Should-fix 5 solves this. Otherwise, correct the brief.
11. **[wedding-site-standards §8] The terminal's address isn't tap-to-map** (story.tsx:163). → Link it to the venue maps deep link used elsewhere.
12. **[input] Rapid Next presses are dropped.** Two quick presses advance one stop, because `go(at.index + 1)` reads stale state (StoryRide.tsx:324). → Keep a pending-target ref.
13. **[a11y minor] The platform is a tab stop even when it doesn't scroll** (StoryRide.tsx:294). → Make it focusable only when it overflows.

## Consider
- The train marker transitions `top`/`left` (ride.css:542, :860). Use `transform`.
- Lanes use `vw` (ride.css:610, :619, :757), which is relative to the viewport, not the 1120 px window on desktop. → Container-query units on `.bd-ride__window`.
- A "departure" hairline in the sign that fills across the dwell. Right now about 270 px of scroll produces no visible feedback.
- "End of the line" appears twice at the terminal (plate and sign).
- "Ride the Sara / + Tyler Line" breaks before "+" at 390 → `Sara&nbsp;+&nbsp;Tyler` (story.tsx:200).
- `nowrap; ellipsis` on map and sign names (ride.css:314-319, :702-704) will truncate real Paired titles. Allow 2 lines on desktop.
- CSS scroll-driven animations (`animation-timeline: scroll()` with keyframe holds as plateaus) could take the JS listener out of the frame loop.
- The `italic-serif-display` waiver text ("the only italic display on the site") is now stale.
- Hallmark's "italic headers" and "eyebrow density" tells conflict with DESIGN.md, which specifies italic card titles and waives kickers. DESIGN.md wins, so these are not scored.

## What is working (keep)
- **The idea.** Their story as their own 'L' line is exactly the "one strong idea that is theirs" in the rubric, and the vocabulary is right: This is / Next stop / Transfer here / End of the line / The Loop 07 · 17 · 27.
- **The 1440 car card is the best element on the page.** Station names in their line colour at 17 px, hollow dumbbells at transfers, the train marker, the current station highlighted.
- **Tokens are clean.** 0 raw hex or `font-family` literals. Every line colour is a DESIGN.md token: plates ≥ 5.36:1, station names ≥ 5.5:1, gilt kicker on ink 8.2:1. `design:lint` 0/0; stylelint clean.
- **axe: 0 violations** on load (mobile/tablet/desktop projects), mid-ride at 390/810/1440, and in reduced motion.
- **The fallbacks work.** Reduced motion, no-JS and print all go flat (print verified via `beforeprint`). The `#starved-rock` deep link lands at p = 11. CLS ≤ 0.0003. Native scroll with no hijack.
- **Nothing is invented.** Every station traces to brief §2, placeholders say so, stand-ins are labelled, and the weekday is derived from the date. Composition change is recorded as PX-25.
