## Two we are not asking you to answer

- **Face grouping in photos.** Built, switched off, and staying off. Illinois
  has a specific law about biometric data (BIPA) and turning this on needs a
  lawyer's sign-off and a retention period you choose — not a setting we flip.
- **Anything a vendor owns.** Your planner's design materials and your
  photographers' images belong to them. The site describes and links; it never
  copies.

## What the site does today, with nothing filled in

The whole platform is built and working: guests claim their invitation with a
code sent to the address you have on file, RSVP for their household, see their
own table when you publish seating, plan travel, claim their ride credit,
upload photos after the day, and ask questions of a concierge that answers
**only** from what you have entered. Asked something you have not answered, it
says so — *"I don't have that information yet ... you can always reach Sara and
Tyler directly"* — rather than inventing one. There are two complete designs to
choose between, switchable from any page.

None of that is waiting on more code. It is waiting on the twenty answers
above, and on nothing else.
