Explore CAA

A building worth arriving early for

The Chicago Athletic Association Hotel, 12 S Michigan Ave — built 1893, and the reason we chose it.

The short history

  • Built in 1893 for the private Chicago Athletic Association.
  • Designed by Henry Ives Cobb, with a facade by Louis Christian Mullgardt.
  • Venetian Gothic, after the Doge's Palace in Venice.
  • It opened amid the 1893 World's Columbian Exposition.
  • A men-only club until 1972.
  • The club closed in 2007.
  • Restored and reopened as a hotel: architecture by Hartshorne Plunkard Architecture, interiors by Roman & Williams.
  • A contributing building in the Historic Michigan Boulevard District.

Look for this

✓Patterned brick on the exterior.
✓Carved limestone.
✓Arched windows, the Venetian Gothic tell.
✓Custom stained glass.
✓Ornate plaster ceilings and cornices.
✓Mosaic and marble floors.
