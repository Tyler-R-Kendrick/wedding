Before you book

One ticket, or two?

It's the difference that costs people their weekend, and airline search results rarely spell it out.

Connection on one ticket

Safer

If a delay makes you miss the connection, the airline rebooks you.

Separate tickets (self-transfer)

Riskier

Miss the second flight and it's your problem, not the airline's. You'd be rebooking and re-paying yourself.

Prices move. Refresh before you book; the price the provider shows at checkout is the one that counts.

Cheaper itineraries are usually the self-transfer ones. The site shows both and explains the trade, rather than sorting by price and letting a guest find out at O'Hare.
