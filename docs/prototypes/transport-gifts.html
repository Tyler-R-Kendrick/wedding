When we can't release one

An admin-assigned entitlement is necessary but not sufficient — the personal question is answered separately, at claim time.

Why it's two checks

The entitlement row says a benefit exists. The fact source answers whether this guest may take it: are they an adult, are they still in that household. Either alone would be a bug — an entitlement that outlived a household change would hand a stranger a code.

Each message says what to do next. None of them exposes whether another guest has a benefit.

Copy constraints

Three things the gifts page may never say

Asserted by tests over the rendered page, not left to an author's judgement.

/cash\s*fund/i

"Cash fund" turns a gift into a transaction. The page says "help us with our next adventures".

/\bdonat(e|ion|ions)\b/i

"Donate" is charity vocabulary. A wedding gift is not a donation.

/\$\s?\d/

No amount, ever — not a suggestion, not a default, not a tier. Naming a number tells a guest what they're worth.

Presence first. The registry exists because guests ask for it, and it stays below the thank-you in emphasis on every variant of the page.
