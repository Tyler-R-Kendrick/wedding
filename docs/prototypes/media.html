The pipeline

Nine states, one direction

Every asset walks this path. Nothing skips ahead, and only one state is visible to a guest.

quarantined

Just landed. Nothing has looked at it yet, and nothing can read it.

validating

Is it really the image or video it claims to be?

processing

Derivatives are generated: thumb, gallery, web-full, poster, video-web.

private

Ready, and visible only to the guest who uploaded it and to admins.

published

The only state a guest sees in the gallery.

hidden

Pulled back from the gallery. Reversible — published and hidden swap freely.

rejected

Not acceptable. Terminal.

failed

Processing broke. Terminal, and it shows up in metrics.

deleted

Gone from storage. Terminal.

And the upload itself

pending → completed, or aborted, expired, rejected. An upload that never completes expires on its own rather than holding a signed URL open forever.
